# Design System - Accountability Tracker

## Product Context
- **What this is:** A native mobile accountability app for private friend groups and personal daily commitments.
- **Who it's for:** Individual consumers who invite friends or small groups to keep daily promises visible.
- **Space/industry:** Consumer wellness, habit tracking, social accountability.
- **Project type:** Native mobile app.

## Aesthetic Direction
- **Direction:** Calm Momentum.
- **Decoration level:** Intentional, warm, and premium without becoming playful.
- **Mood:** The app should feel like a quiet coach and a trusted friend group: focused, encouraging, and socially present.

## Typography
- **Display/Hero:** System rounded/heavy weights until custom font loading is introduced.
- **Body:** System sans, optimized for native readability.
- **UI/Labels:** Uppercase micro-labels with generous letter spacing.
- **Scale:** Display 36, title 28, section 21, body 16, meta 13.

## Color
- **Approach:** Warm neutrals with high-contrast ink and restrained semantic color.
- **Primary:** `#10231F` deep ink, used for primary actions and hero text.
- **Secondary:** `#2F7D73` teal, used for progress and success.
- **Accent:** `#F2A65A` amber, used for deadline energy.
- **Neutrals:** `#FBF7EF`, `#F4EDE1`, `#E8DCCB`, `#6F675C`, `#171411`.
- **Semantic:** success `#147D64`, warning `#B66A1E`, error `#B42318`, info `#2E6E9E`.

## Spacing
- **Base unit:** 4px.
- **Density:** Comfortable.
- **Scale:** xs 4, sm 8, md 16, lg 24, xl 32, 2xl 48.

## Layout
- **Approach:** Card-forward native layout with strong top summaries and calm whitespace.
- **Surfaces:** Warm app background, elevated cream cards, soft borders, large rounded corners.
- **Border radius:** sm 10, md 16, lg 24, xl 32, full 999.

## Motion
- **Approach:** Minimal-functional for MVP.
- **Future:** Add subtle check-in success feedback and feed item reveals once core QA is stable.
